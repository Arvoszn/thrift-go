Thrift Go

Privacy

Thrift Go is operated by Spawntel. Updated September 30, 2026.

This policy covers shop accounts connected to Thrift Go and the people who bid on those shops’ drops or message them about orders.

Information used

When a shop connects, Thrift Go stores its authorized Facebook Page and linked Instagram professional account identifiers, account names, access tokens, and connection status. It reads posts selected for drops and comments on those posts.

To run a sale, Thrift Go stores bids, commenter identifiers and usernames, comment identifiers, messages, receipt references and attachment links, payment review results, and shipping information provided by the buyer or seller. Shop settings include the receiving GCash account and shipping charges.

How it is used

These records match bids to the correct shop and post, select winners and runner-up buyers, notify buyers through Meta, let the seller review payments, and record fulfillment. A receipt or reference submitted by a buyer is a payment claim. The seller checks their own GCash account before confirming payment.

Storage and access

Records are stored on the operator’s server. Account tokens and login sessions are encrypted at rest. An authenticated shop can access its own workspace. The operator can maintain the service and help resolve order or connection problems. Order records and messages are not sent to an AI model by this workflow.

Facebook and Instagram handle account authorization and message delivery under Meta’s privacy policy. Thrift Go does not sell these records or use them to train models.

Retention and your choices

Sale records remain available for the seller’s order history until removed by the operator. There is currently no automatic retention cutoff. Shops can disconnect their account in Shop settings to remove the app’s stored token and stop further processing for that account. They can also remove Spawn Thrift access in Meta’s business integration settings.

To request a copy, correction, or deletion of your information, contact the shop you bought from or email info@spawntel.com. Include the shop and the relevant order or message so the operator can locate the record and verify the request.
